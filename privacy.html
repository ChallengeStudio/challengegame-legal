4. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Contact Us

6. Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data from ChallengeGame. Please note that account deletion is not immediate and will be finalized after a 30-day waiting period.

Option 1: Delete from within the app

  1. Open ChallengeGame
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account
  3. Confirm deletion request
  4. Your account will be marked for deletion and permanently deleted after 30 days

To cancel the deletion: Log in to your account before the 30-day period ends and choose Cancel Account Deletion in pop up. Your account and data will be restored immediately.

Option 2: Email request

Send an email to support@challengegame.com with the subject line "Delete My Account". Include the username or email address associated with your account. Your account will be marked for deletion and permanently removed after 30 days.

To cancel the deletion: Email support@challengegame.com with the subject line "Cancel Account Deletion" before the 30-day period ends. Include your username or email address. We will cancel the deletion request and restore your account immediately.

What gets deleted

  • Account information (username, email)
  • Game history and statistics
  • Currency balances and rewards
  • Friends list and social data
  • All other personal data tied to your account

Note: Some aggregated, anonymized analytics data may be retained for product improvement but cannot be linked back to you.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@challengegame.com
